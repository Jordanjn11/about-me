My Data Analysis Process

1 - work with stakeholders to understand their business questions and how they plan to use the analysis insights.
2 - identify sources of data, collaborating w/ key SMEs and functional leaders across the business as needed
3 - query, extract, mine, export, or scrape the sources for the data I need 
4 - export and catalogue data into Excel or Google Sheets / then on to PowerQuery
5 - look for data quality problems or outliers
6 - analyze the data with basic stats and viz
7 - use pretty basic charts to visualize data
8 - communicate MVP insights with stakeholders
9 - advocate for action/recommend further analysis or next steps with stakeholders based on my findings
10 - iterate on MVP analysis and reports based on stakeholder feedback
